Models

Connect them all.

Kern has no model of its own. You plug in the ones you use, and pick a different one for each window.

Three ways to connect a model

01

On your computer

Kern finds Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM and llama.cpp running on this machine and lists what each has loaded. You can add your own OpenAI compatible server too; only this computer's addresses are accepted.

02

With a key

Save a key under Connections for Google Gemini, Mistral, Hugging Face, OpenCode Zen or OpenRouter. OpenRouter is one key for many models, including Kimi, Grok and Llama.

03

With tools you have

Already use the Claude or Codex command line tools? Kern bridges to them locally, so Claude and GPT models are a click away. Providers you configured elsewhere appear too.

Why it matters

Use the right model for the job.

Compare without switching apps

Put the same task in two windows with two models and see which does it better, side by side.

Keep going when one is down

Rate limited or offline? Open another window on the same project with a different model.

Match cost to the task

Use a local or cheap model for routine edits and a frontier model for the hard problem.

Not locked in

Your projects, keys and conversations live on your computer, not with any one provider.
