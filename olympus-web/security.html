Commands ask separately

Choose where your work goes

Use a model on your computer to keep its processing local. If you choose a cloud provider, your requests go to that provider.

No activity tracking

Olympus does not collect usage analytics or automatically send crash reports. You do not need an Olympus account.

A little care goes a long way.

AI can make mistakes. Review proposed changes and commands before approving them, and keep a backup of important work.

Your chosen AI tools and providers have their own privacy practices. Check those when deciding which model to use.
