Requirements

Ready for your computer.

A short checklist before you start.

Home/Requirements

What you need.

  • A supported computer. macOS, Windows 10 or 11, or 64-bit Linux.
  • A compatible coding agent. Have your chosen agent ready to use, including any setup it requires.
  • Your choice of model provider. Use a local model or a cloud provider supported by your chosen setup.
  • A project folder. Choose the work you want to open in Olympus.

Choose your download.

macOS

Separate downloads are available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Choose the one that matches your computer.

Windows

For Windows 10 and Windows 11 on a 64-bit computer.

Linux

For 64-bit Linux. The download is an AppImage.

Your model

Speed and memory needs depend on the AI model you choose. Check the requirements in your model app.

Choose a download

Version compatibility.

Compatibility depends on the agent, model, and versions you choose. Try the free release with your setup before purchasing.

If you have trouble getting started, check your installed version and the release notes.
