Requirements

Ready for your computer.

A short checklist before you start.

Home/Requirements

What you need.

  • A supported computer. The current development build is tested on Windows. See the planned platforms below before expecting support for another system.
  • OpenCode installed. Olympus starts the OpenCode command-line agent for each workspace. Its executable must be available on your computer.
  • Your choice of model provider. Use a local model or a cloud provider supported by your chosen setup.
  • A project folder. Choose the work you want to open in Olympus.

Planned platforms.

macOS

Apple Silicon and Intel builds are planned. Public downloads are not available yet.

Windows

Development is being tested on 64-bit Windows. Public release requirements will be confirmed before launch.

Linux

A 64-bit Linux AppImage is planned. Compatibility will be confirmed before a public release.

Your model

Speed and memory needs depend on the AI model you choose. Check the requirements in your model app.

Check availability

Version compatibility.

Compatibility depends on the agent, model, and versions you choose. Join the waitlist and we will send a build when one is ready.

If you have trouble getting started, check your installed version and the release notes.
