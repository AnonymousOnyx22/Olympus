How it works

  1. 1

    Get ready

    Make sure your computer and model are ready. The setup checklist covers what you need.

  2. 2

    Choose your model

    Choose a model from the list. Start your local model app first, or select a provider you have already connected.

  3. 3

    Open a project

    Choose a folder from your computer. Olympus keeps the project and its conversations together.

  4. 4

    Review, then run

    Describe a task, review the proposed changes, and accept the ones you want. Then run your project to see the result.

Before you start. Check the requirements and open the model app you want to use.
