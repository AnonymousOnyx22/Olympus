What Olympus actually is

One screen. Every project you're working on, each with its own agent.

Olympus is a multi-agent harness, not a single chat window. Open three projects and you get three independent agents, each with its own folder, its own conversation, and its own running work — all visible on one screen at once. Switching projects never interrupts the one you left.

Each project keeps its own model, its own permission mode, and its own history. Nothing here is a tab pretending to be a project — every pane is a fully separate agent, running at the same time as the others.

From setup to your first project.

Prepare your model, choose a folder, and start a conversation. Review changes as you go.

  1. 1

    Get ready

    Make sure your computer and model are ready. The setup checklist covers what you need.

  2. 2

    Choose your model

    Choose a model from the list. Start your local model app first, or select a provider you have already connected.

  3. 3

    Open a project

    Choose a folder from your computer. Olympus keeps the project and its conversations together.

  4. 4

    Review, then run

    Describe a task, review the proposed changes, and accept the ones you want. Then run your project to see the result.

Before you start. Check the requirements and open the model app you want to use.
