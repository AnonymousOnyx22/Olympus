2. What the app collects

Olympus does not collect usage analytics, track your activity, or automatically send crash reports. You do not need an account to use the app.

We do not collect your project files or prompts. Your chosen AI provider may receive them when you use its service, as explained below.

3. Where the app’s own data lives

Your settings and app data are stored on your computer.

This folder stores your project list, preferences, window settings, licence key, and local logs. Your project files stay in the folders you chose.

Deleting the app does not delete this directory. That is deliberate, because reinstalling should not mean rebuilding your project list, but it does mean the data outlives the install. Remove it by hand when you want it gone. Log files may contain fragments of terminal output, including paths and error text from commands you approved.

4. What we hold about you, as a customer

Only what a purchase requires:

  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • The transaction reference and amount, from our payment processor.
  • The date of purchase, and which version line the licence covers.
  • Anything you have chosen to send us in a support email, which is kept as long as it takes to resolve the matter and no longer.

We do not build profiles, we do not buy lists, and we do not share or sell customer data. Payment card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and never reach us. [PLACEHOLDER: name the payment processor here once it is chosen, and state its own data terms.]

5. When something does leave your machine

Your chosen model, provider, or project may make connections of its own. The following explains the main situations.

  • Your chosen AI model. The agent shares your prompts and relevant project content with the model you choose. When the model runs on your computer, that processing stays on your computer.
  • A cloud AI provider. If you choose a cloud provider, your prompts and relevant project content are sent to it. That provider’s privacy terms apply.
  • Your project preview. Running a project may connect to services that the project itself uses.

The agents and model providers you connect have their own privacy practices. Olympus does not control how those third-party tools handle your data.

6. What “local-first” does and does not mean here

It means Olympus does not collect activity data or require an account. You can work offline when your chosen AI model and project also support it.

It does not mean:

  • That every AI tool you choose works offline. Check the settings and privacy practices of your chosen tools.
  • That every connection is encrypted. Olympus does not make that promise.
  • That the agent is safe to leave unattended. It is a program running with your permissions, and approval is a human process.
  • Your chosen tools have their own policies. Check how your agent and model provider handle your data.

7. This website

This website uses Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to load the fonts, which shares your IP address with Google. The website does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

The interactive notes and weekend planner examples save what you enter in this browser so it is available when you return. These notes and plans are not sent to us or to an AI provider. You can remove individual entries in the examples, or clear this website's browser data to remove everything saved by them.

8. Your rights

Because the data we hold is a purchase record, your rights are mostly about access and correction. Email {{contact.supportEmail}} to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will answer, and we will do it without asking you to prove anything beyond that you are the person who bought it.

Deleting the app’s local data is entirely under your control and needs no request: remove the app data described in section 3. [PLACEHOLDER: statutory rights, supervisory authority and complaint procedure must be added here to match the selling jurisdiction before launch.]

9. Children

Olympus is a developer tool that runs a coding agent with your permissions. It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

10. Changes

This policy may change, in a later version published on this page with its own effective date. Because the app collects nothing and this site sets no cookies, a change here cannot retroactively affect data already gathered from your use of the software.

If you find a claim on this page that the software does not honour, that is a bug with legal weight. Report it at {{contact.securityEmail}} or {{contact.supportEmail}} and it will be treated as the first thing to fix.
