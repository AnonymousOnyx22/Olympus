What Olympus actually is

One screen. Every project you're working on, each with its own agent.

Olympus is a multi-agent harness, not a single chat window. Open three projects and you get three independent agents, each with its own folder, its own conversation, and its own running work — all visible at once. Switching projects never interrupts the one you left.

Before you settle in

A few good questions.

The practical details, so you know what to expect.

Read the setup guide ↗

What do I need to get started?

A supported computer, a project folder, and a compatible agent and model ready to use. The setup checklist covers the software you need.

Does everything run on my computer?

With a model running on your computer, your AI work stays there. If you choose a cloud provider, your requests go to that provider. Olympus does not track your activity or require an account.

Can I use it now?

No. Nothing is released yet, and nothing is for sale. Olympus is in development, and builds go to the waitlist first. What a licence will cost, and what it will include, has not been decided. See the complete pricing terms.

What can the agent change without asking?

Proposed file edits wait for your approval. Commands ask separately because they can also change files. Review both before accepting. Learn about your controls.

Which models work with Olympus?

Olympus is independent of any one provider. Use a model supported by your chosen agent and setup, whether it runs locally or through a connected cloud provider.

What if it isn’t the right fit?

Join the waitlist and you will be told when a build is ready. Any refund window will be published before the first sale, not after it. The refund policy explains the process.
