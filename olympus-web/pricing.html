What is in the box

What you are buying, in full

Written out so you can hold us to it. If something here is not true, that is a refund, and it is worth telling us about either way.

Every platform, from one licence

macOS on Apple Silicon, macOS on Intel, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux x64. Four builds, one price, no per-platform upgrade. Two of them may be installed on the same licence at once.

Every update inside 0.x

Bug fixes and features, for as long as the 0.x line exists. Not a subscription. You already own those builds. When 0.x ends, your 0.x build stops receiving updates and keeps working.

Commercial use

Use it for paid work, on client repositories, inside an agency. The licence is per person and follows the person: change employer, move country, change laptop, and it is still yours.

Support by email

support@example.com, answered by whoever maintains the app. There is no queue behind a support plan, because there is no support plan. Bugs get fixed in public in the changelog.

What is not included

  • Redistribution. You may not publish the binary, put it in a template, a bundle, a public mirror or a CI artefact other people can pull. A licence is not a distribution right.
  • Your model server. Whatever Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM or llama.cpp costs you in electricity and hardware is between you and them. Olympus does not resell inference.
  • opencode. A separate project with its own licence, which you install yourself from its own sources.
  • Hosting, accounts or sync. There are none, which is why there is nothing to charge for monthly.

What a future major version costs

There is no 1.0 yet, so there is no number to quote. Inventing one would be dishonest, and you should be suspicious of any product that quotes a price for software that does not exist.

  • A one-off upgrade, not a second subscription. Paid once when it ships.
  • Less than $49. It is an upgrade of something you already own.
  • Published before it ships, on this page and in the changelog.
  • 0.x builds keep working. Your 0.x licence and your 0.x binaries stay valid, against the opencode versions they worked with, indefinitely.

More than one person

Teams and quantity

The licence is per person, so five people is five licences. There is no team tier and no per-seat management console, because there is no server to run one on.

  • Two to nine seats. $49 per person, no discount and no minimum commitment. Email support@example.com with the number and the platform split and you will get an invoice you can pay by bank transfer.
  • Ten seats and up. Same price per person. At this size a single point of contact is worth having, so tell us who it is and they will get the release notes first.
  • No floating licences. A licence is a person, not a concurrent session. If two people share one login, you have bought one licence and used two.
  • No procurement portal. There is no vendor form, no security questionnaire queue and no SOC 2 report, because there is no company behind this yet. If you need one, ask and you will get an honest answer about what cannot be provided.

Refunds. Fourteen days from the purchase, whatever the seat count, no questions and no form. The refund terms are here, and the licence is the version that binds.
