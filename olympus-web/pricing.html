What is in the box

What you are buying, in full

Written out so you can hold us to it. If something here is not true, that is a refund, and it is worth telling us about either way.

Every platform, from one licence

macOS on Apple Silicon, macOS on Intel, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux x64. Four builds, one price, no per-platform upgrade. Two of them may be installed on the same licence at once.

Every update inside 0.x

Bug fixes and features, for as long as the 0.x line exists. Not a subscription. You already own those builds. When 0.x ends, your 0.x build stops receiving updates and keeps working.

Commercial use

Use it for paid work, on client repositories, inside an agency. The licence is for one person and follows the person: change employer, move country, change laptop, and it is still yours.

Support by email

{{contact.supportEmail}}, answered by whoever maintains the app. There is no queue behind a support plan, because there is no support plan. Bugs get fixed in public in the changelog.

What is not included

  • Sharing the app. A licence is for your own use. It does not let you redistribute or resell Olympus.
  • Your AI model. Any model subscriptions, usage charges, or hardware costs are separate.
  • Agents and providers. Any third-party tools or subscriptions you choose are separate from your Olympus licence.
  • Hosting, accounts or sync. There are none, which is why there is nothing to charge for monthly.

What a future major version costs

There is no 1.0 yet, so there is no number to quote. Inventing one would be dishonest, and you should be suspicious of any product that quotes a price for software that does not exist.

  • A one-off upgrade, not a second subscription. Paid once when it ships.
  • Less than the licence price, once there is one. It is an upgrade of something you already own.
  • Published before it ships, on this page and in the changelog.
  • Your licence stays yours. You may keep using your purchased 0.x release. Compatibility with third-party tools can change as those tools are updated.

More than one person

Teams and quantity

Each person needs their own licence. Five people means five licences.

  • Two to nine seats. One price, with no minimum commitment. The amount is not set. Email {{contact.supportEmail}} with the number and the platform split and you will get an invoice you can pay by bank transfer.
  • Ten seats and up. The same price for one person. At this size a single point of contact is worth having, so tell us who it is and they will get the release notes first.
  • No floating licences. A licence is a person, not a concurrent session. If two people share one login, you have bought one licence and used two.
  • Business requirements. Contact support before purchasing if your organisation needs particular documents or assurances.

Refunds. A stated refund window from the purchase, whatever the seat count, no questions and no form. The refund terms are here, and the Licence is the version that binds.
